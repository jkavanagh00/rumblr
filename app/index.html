The User Journey

Sign up

Create your account and join the rumble. Make sure not to use your real name!

Respond to Statments

We'll show you some controversial statements, you tell us whether you agree and how important your opinion is to you.

Find Mismatches

Using your responses, we'll mismatch you with users with vastly different points of view, based on your mismatch percentage.

Issue Challenges

When a mismatch catches your eye (and your ire) it's time to send them a challenge! Challenges can be either green, orange or red, reflecting different levels of moderation.

Time to Rumble!

Go head-to-head in a private, one-on-one bout.

Throw in the Towel

Either you or your opponent can end the bout at any time, no questions asked.

Block & Report

If you're sick of hearing from a user, just block them, and if they've violated the terms of your bout, send us a report.

Introduction

RUMBLR is the world's first and only "online hating" site, where users respond to controversial statements and get "mismatched" with other users with opposing views. If your mismatch accepts your challenge, the two of you can join a private, one-on-one rumble in which either user can end the bout at any time.
